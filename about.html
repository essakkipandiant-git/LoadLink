About LoadLink

India's most efficient transport marketplace

Our Mission

LoadLink was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to eliminate inefficiency in India's transport industry. Every day, thousands of trucks run empty on return trips while shopkeepers struggle to find affordable, reliable transport. We bridge this gap with technology.

Our Story

Started in 2024 by a team of logistics enthusiasts and tech innovators, LoadLink grew from a small pilot in Mumbai to serve 25+ cities across India. Our founders saw firsthand how brokers and middlemen were eating into drivers' earnings and inflating costs for shopkeepers.

We built LoadLink to give power back to the people who move India's economy — the drivers and the small business owners. No commissions, no middlemen, just direct connections.
