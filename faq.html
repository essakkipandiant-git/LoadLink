Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LoadLink

General

LoadLink is India's most efficient transport marketplace. We connect truck drivers with shopkeepers who need to move goods. Drivers can find loads for their routes, and shopkeepers can find trucks heading to their destination — all without any middleman or commission.

Yes! LoadLink is completely free. We charge zero commissions and zero fees for posting vehicles, loads, or connecting with other users. Our mission is to empower the transport community.

Traditional brokers charge 5-10% commission and control the negotiation. LoadLink removes the middleman entirely. You post directly, find matches in real-time, and negotiate directly with the other party. This means more earnings for drivers and lower costs for shopkeepers.

For Drivers

Visit the Load Board to see all available loads. You can filter by pickup area, destination, goods type, and weight to find loads that match your route and vehicle capacity.

Go to Post Vehicle, fill in your vehicle details (type, capacity), pickup area, destination, and your contact number. Your listing will appear on the Load Board instantly for shopkeepers to see.

For Shopkeepers

You can either post your load on the Post Load page so drivers can find you, or browse the Load Board to find drivers already heading to your destination.

Once you find a matching driver, contact them directly using the phone number shown on their listing. You negotiate rates, timing, and terms directly — no middleman or platform interference.

All drivers register with their mobile number and provide vehicle details. We're continuously working on enhancing our verification process with license checks and community ratings to ensure safety and trust.

Account & Technical
