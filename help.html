Getting Started

Creating an Account

Sign up as a Driver or Shopkeeper. Go to Register, pick your role, enter your mobile number and details, and you're ready to go.

Posting a Vehicle

Go to Post Vehicle, fill in your vehicle type, pickup area, destination, and contact details. Your listing goes live instantly.

Posting a Load

Go to Post Load, describe your goods, pickup/drop locations, and weight. Drivers will see your listing on the Load Board.

Finding Matches

Visit the Load Board to see all live postings. Filter by route, vehicle type, or weight to find your perfect match.
